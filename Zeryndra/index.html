About

Zeryndra is a top-down tile-based open world RPG being built from the ground up by a single developer. The game aims to combine classic RPG elements — exploration, story, and progression — with skilling and management systems inspired by games like Old School RuneScape and classic JRPGs.

The world of Zeryndra is an open world with hand-crafted regions to explore, each with its own feel, encounters, and secrets. Players will be able to choose their path: pursue the main story, grind skills, manage resources, or simply explore.

Everything is being built from scratch in Godot, with all art created in Aseprite. As a solo developer, the scope is intentionally kept manageable — but the ambition is high.

Planned Features
🗺️
Open World
A hand-crafted tile-based world with multiple regions, secrets, and areas to discover at your own pace.
📖
Story & Progression
A narrative-driven experience with character development and quests. The story follows a single timeline — no branching choices, just a focused narrative.
⚔️
Combat
Combat system currently in design — deciding between turn-based and live-action approaches.
🪓
Skilling
Train a variety of skills — gathering, crafting, and more — to grow stronger and unlock new content.
🏗️
Management Systems
Build, upgrade, and manage structures or systems that support your progression and world-building.
🎨
Hand-Crafted Pixel Art
All sprites, tiles, and animations created from scratch using Aseprite. Targeting a consistent 32×32 tile pixel art style throughout.
Open Design Decisions
🤔
Combat System: Turn-Based vs. Live Action
One of the biggest open questions in early development. Turn-based combat fits the classic RPG feel and is more approachable to design solo, while live-action combat would feel more dynamic and immersive but adds significant complexity to both design and implementation. This decision will shape a lot of downstream systems and will be made once the core world movement and tilemap systems are stable.

The Developer Journey
This is a learning project as much as a game project. Building Zeryndra means picking up new skills along the way — pixel art from scratch, animation principles, world design, and Godot's GDScript ecosystem. The scope is kept intentionally lean at this stage: get the core loop working, learn the tools, and let the design evolve naturally.

Progress, art previews, and devlogs will be shared on Discord, YouTube, and X/Twitter as development moves forward.
